Software is a human activity

As we see more and more advances in machine learning it becomes tempting to imagine that complex software systems can be created rapidly by language models. The truth is that even simple software systems like this website get more complicated the moment a human interacts with them. For example maybe the website you are building looks great on your laptop but it becomes unusable when someone tries to look at it on a phone or if someone with color blindness looks at it.

Complexity is inherent to human life and intentional software can help us manage that complexity by careful managing the details of our digital interactions. Unfortunately when software is written by language models the details are not handled with intention. Code written by a langauge model is statistically likely to look correct and impressive. It often works flawlessly for the people who made it only to become buggy an unusable when used in slightly different ways or under slightly different circumstances. The reasons for this are often nuanced and surprising.
